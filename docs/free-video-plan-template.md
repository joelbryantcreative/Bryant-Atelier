# Free Video Plan: how to make one

Target: **45 minutes or less per plan**, sent within 2 business days. Not published on the website (this folder isn't part of the build).

---

## 1. Research (15 min)

Look at the business the way a customer would. Note anything that makes you go "oh, that's interesting".

- [ ] **Website:** what they say they do, who it's for, any founder or "about" story
- [ ] **Instagram:** last 12 posts. What gets the most likes or comments? Is there any video? Who is on camera?
- [ ] **Google reviews:** what customers praise in their own words (this is often the real story)
- [ ] **Ads:** check the Meta Ad Library (facebook.com/ads/library) for their name
- [ ] **Their industry:** find 2 or 3 businesses like theirs (anywhere in the world) doing video well. Note what they do: who's on camera, the format, the hook
- [ ] **The form:** what they're interested in, budget, and anything they told you

Write down:
- **The person:** who's behind it, and why they started
- **The place:** where it happens, and what it looks, sounds and feels like
- **The proof:** what customers keep saying in reviews
- **The gap:** what they're not showing that they should be

---

## 2. The one-page plan (20 min)

Copy this into a Google Doc or Canva page with your branding, and export as PDF.

> **[Business name]: your video plan**
> Made by Joel Bryant, Bryant Atelier. [Date]
>
> **1. Your story in one line**
> [One sentence. The angle that makes them worth watching.]
> *Example shape: "A fourth-generation chef bringing her family's table from Abruzzo to Byron Bay."*
>
> **How I'd film it:** [2 or 3 sentences: the setting, who's on camera, the feeling. Brand film, founder portrait or documentary.]
>
> **2. Three short-form ideas for this month**
>
> | | Idea | Opening hook (first 2 seconds) | Shoot it |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | 1 | [What happens] | "[The first line spoken or shown on screen]" | [Phone or pro, where, who] |
> | 2 | | | |
> | 3 | | | |
>
> **3. What others in your industry do well**
> - [Business, with a link]: [what they do well, in one line]
> - [Business, with a link]: [what they do well]
> - **What you can borrow:** [one idea]
>
> **4. What's holding your video back**
> - [Honest note 1, e.g. "No faces: people follow people, and your team never appears."]
> - [Honest note 2]
> - [Quick win they can do this week]
>
> *(Not posting yet? Replace this section with "Where to start": the first 3 posts in order.)*
>
> **Want help making it?** Reply to this email or book a call: [link]. No pressure. The plan is yours either way.

**Hook formulas that work for short-form:**
- *Behind the curtain:* "What it actually takes to [thing they do]"
- *The number:* "[X] years / [X] hands / [X] hours in one [product]"
- *The objection:* "Everyone thinks [myth]. Here's what really happens."
- *The person:* "Meet [name]. They've been [doing X] for [Y] years."
- *The before and after:* show the end result first, then rewind

---

## 3. The video walkthrough (10 min)

Record your screen and face (Loom, or QuickTime plus screen recording). Aim for 8 to 10 minutes. Don't script it word for word.

1. **Hi [name] (30 sec):** what you looked at, and what stood out first.
2. **Your story (2 min):** say the one line, explain why, and show the reviews or posts that prove it.
3. **The 3 ideas (3 min):** for each one, say the hook out loud and describe the shot.
4. **Who's doing it well (1 min):** show one or two examples from their industry.
5. **What's holding you back (2 min):** scroll their Instagram or site and point at specifics. Be kind and direct.
6. **Close (30 sec):** "The plan's yours either way. If you want me to shoot it, reply and we'll book a call."

---

## 5. Track it

Keep a simple sheet: date, business, budget band, interest, time taken, reply (Y/N), booked call (Y/N), became a client (Y/N), value.

After 10 plans you'll know your conversion rate and average time per plan. Those are the real numbers for deciding whether to keep it free, cap it per month, or show a "usually $X" price.
